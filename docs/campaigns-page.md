# Campaigns

Southall Stories reports on campaigns as part of the public record: what residents were asking for, what institutions promised, what changed, and what happened in the end.

Some campaigns are active. Others have concluded, succeeded in part, failed, or moved into a different phase. This page keeps those outcomes visible rather than leaving old calls to action hanging around after events have moved on.

## Current and continuing campaigns

### Clean air, pollution and environmental health

Southall has lived for years with disputes over air quality, contaminated land, industrial emissions and the health impacts of major development.

Southall Stories continues to follow the evidence, official monitoring, regulatory decisions and community responses around pollution and public health.

### Local democracy and accountability

Campaigning around petitions, consultations, public meetings, planning, council scrutiny and access to information continues across Southall and Ealing.

The focus here is less on promoting a single campaign than on documenting how decisions are made, what residents are told, and whether public bodies follow through.

## Campaigns that have concluded

### Save Ealing’s Children’s Centres

The campaign against Ealing Council’s planned closure and reorganisation of children’s centres raised substantial public support and funded a legal challenge.

That legal challenge has now concluded. The High Court found in favour of Ealing Council, with Mr Justice Kimblin rejecting the challenge.

Southall Stories will keep the reporting and campaign material in the archive as part of the public record, but the old urgent fundraising and action calls should be treated as historical rather than current.

[Browse reporting on children’s centres and the campaign →](/categories/campaigns/)

## What counts as a campaign here?

Southall Stories is a reporting publication, not a campaign organisation. Coverage may include campaigns that David Marsden has supported or taken part in, but the purpose of this page is to document them accurately over time — including setbacks, court decisions, compromises and outcomes that campaigners did not want.

Where a campaign has ended, the page should say so clearly. Where the position is uncertain or still changing, the reporting should make that clear too.
