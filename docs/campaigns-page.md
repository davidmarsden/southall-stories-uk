# Campaigns

Southall Stories reports on campaigns as part of the public record: what residents were asking for, what institutions promised, what changed, and what happened in the end.

Some campaigns are active. Others have concluded, succeeded in part, failed, or moved into a different phase. This page keeps those outcomes visible without leaving old calls to action hanging around after events have moved on.

It is also a directory. Some of the campaigns and source material linked below were not reported on directly by Southall Stories, but they are useful parts of the local civic record and are kept here for reference.

## Campaigns that have concluded

### Save Ealing’s Children’s Centres

The campaign against Ealing Council’s planned closure and reorganisation of children’s centres raised substantial public support and funded a judicial review challenge.

The legal challenge has now concluded. In **R (JO) v London Borough of Ealing [2026] EWHC 886 (Admin)**, Mr Justice Kimblin dismissed the claim. The judgment found, among other things, that the consultation had been undertaken at a sufficiently formative stage and that the council’s decision was one it was lawfully entitled to make.

The campaign’s fundraising page and legal documents remain useful records of the arguments made, the evidence relied on and the eventual outcome:

- [CrowdJustice: Save Ealing’s Children’s Centres](https://www.crowdjustice.com/case/save-ealings-childrens-centres/)
- [Local Government Lawyer: High Court rejects challenge to proposed closure of children’s centres](https://localgovernmentlawyer.co.uk/education-law/394-education-news/100268-high-court-rejects-challenge-to-proposed-closure-of-children-s-centres)
- [BAILII: R (JO) v London Borough of Ealing [2026] EWHC 886 (Admin)](https://www.bailii.org/ew/cases/EWHC/Admin/2026/886.html)
- [Browse Southall Stories campaign reporting →](/categories/campaigns/)

## What counts as a campaign here?

Southall Stories is a reporting publication, not a campaign organisation. Coverage may include campaigns that David Marsden has supported or taken part in, but the purpose of this page is to document them accurately over time — including setbacks, court decisions, compromises and outcomes that campaigners did not want.

Where a campaign has ended, the page should say so clearly. Where the position is uncertain or still changing, the reporting should make that clear too.

This page also preserves external campaign links where they add useful context to the local record, including campaigns Southall Stories did not itself cover.
