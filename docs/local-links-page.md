# Local links & resources

Southall Stories is one part of a much wider local information network. This page collects useful community sites, local reporting, official sources and archived projects relating to Southall and the London Borough of Ealing.

A link here is not an endorsement of everything a site publishes. The aim is to make useful local sources easier to find and to preserve links to material that might otherwise disappear.

## Southall community voices

- [Southall Speaks](https://atterkalsi.substack.com/) — local writing and commentary by Atter Kalsi.
- [MySouthall](https://southall.substack.com/) — Southall-focused commentary and campaigning.
- [Southall Residents Alliance](https://southallresidentsalliance.co.uk/) — resident campaigning and community updates.
- [Visit Southall](https://www.visitsouthall.co.uk/) — long-running Southall news, events, businesses and local information.

## Ealing community and commentary

- [Ealing Matters](https://ealingmatters.org.uk/) — community news and development information from across the borough.
- [West Ealing Neighbours](https://www.westealingneighbours.org.uk/) — community organisation and local information for West Ealing.
- [The View from W5](https://theviewfromw5.substack.com/) — Ealing-focused reporting and commentary.
- [Vicious Ealing Council](https://vicious-ealing-council.co.uk/) — commentary and campaigning about Ealing Council.
- [Ealing: Rotten Borough](https://ealingrottenborough.wordpress.com/) — long-running commentary on Ealing Council and local politics.

## Local news

- [Ealing.News — Southall](https://www.ealing.news/tag/southall/) — Southall stories from borough-wide local news site Ealing.News.
- [Asian Standard — Southall](https://www.asianstandard.co.uk/category/regional-news/southall/) — Southall coverage from Asian Standard.

## Official and civic sources

- [Your Voice, Your Town — Southall](https://dosomethinggood.ealing.gov.uk/your-voice-your-town/southall/) — Ealing Council's Southall participation pages.
- [Ealing Council meetings and papers](https://ealing.moderngov.co.uk/) — agendas, reports, decisions and council meeting records.
- [WhatDoTheyKnow](https://www.whatdotheyknow.com/) — search public Freedom of Information requests or make your own.
- [Ealing Civic Commons](https://ealing.civiccommons.co.uk/) — a public, connected civic record of places, organisations, decisions, documents and events across Ealing.

## Campaigns and environmental records

- [Stop The Towers](https://stopthetowers.info/) — resident campaigning on development and building height in Ealing.
- [Warren Farm Nature Reserve](https://www.warrenfarmnaturereserve.co.uk/) — campaign information and updates about Warren Farm.
- [Southall Stories campaigns](/campaigns/) — current, concluded and archived campaigns collected by Southall Stories.
