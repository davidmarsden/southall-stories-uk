# Subscribe

Southall Stories is independent local journalism for Southall and the London Borough of Ealing.

Subscribe by email to get new reporting without having to remember to check the site. The newsletter is simply a way to follow the work: no paywall, no members-only version of the reporting, and no advertising list.

## What you’ll get

Subscribers receive new Southall Stories posts by email, including investigations, local-democracy reporting, environment and health stories, campaign updates and other pieces published on the site.

You can unsubscribe at any time.

## Support Southall Stories

Everything on Southall Stories is free to read. The publication has no corporate or political sponsor, and reader support does not buy editorial influence.
