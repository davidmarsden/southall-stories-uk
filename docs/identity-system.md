# Southall Stories identity system

## Core identity

**Masthead**  
SOUTHALL STORIES — black type on Southall Yellow.

**Descriptor**  
Independent local journalism

**Main strapline**  
Recording what happens to Southall — and who is responsible.

**Short line**  
Local people. Local questions.

**Character line**  
Digging the dirt, unearthing the truth.

**Easter egg**  
As read by Ealing Council’s Press Office

## Visual rules

- Keep the yellow/black masthead as the primary visual identifier.
- Do not use an `SS` monogram.
- The old quill is retired from the core identity.
- The original Southall Gasworks “No unauthorised access” image is deliberately retained as the favicon/site icon even though it is stylistically different from the masthead system. Its historical and symbolic value is the reason for the exception.
- Real Southall photography is preferred to generic civic/news imagery.
- Avoid polishing the identity into a generic newspaper or corporate-news look.

## Social and metadata

The default social-preview image is the original Gasworks “No unauthorised access” image. Posts with their own lead image override that default.

Default metadata language:

- Title/mission: `Recording what happens to Southall — and who is responsible.`
- Description: `Independent local journalism from Southall and across Ealing. Local people. Local questions. Digging the dirt, unearthing the truth.`

## Colour references

- Southall Yellow: `#F4C20D` / existing theme yellow equivalent
- Charcoal: approximately `#1B1B1B`
- Paper/light background: approximately `#F6F1E6`
- Mid grey: approximately `#6B6B6B`

Existing live-theme colours should take precedence where minor differences already work well visually.
